How to Play

Simple to pick up. Hard to put down.

👆

Mobile

Touch and drag the bubble anywhere on screen to dodge ice and grab powerups.

⌨️

Desktop

Use all four arrow keys to move freely around the board. Speed and control matter.

🧊

Survive

You get three lives. Each ice cube hit cracks one. Three cracks and you're out.

📈

Level Up

Every 50 points you advance a level. Each level gets 15% faster. How far can you go?

Powerups

Grab a bottle to change the game.

🟠

Orange Rush

Smash ice cubes for +5 points each. The screen pulses orange — go hunting.

🛡️

Cola Shield

One free hit. A dashed ring protects you from the next ice cube that finds you.

🟢

Mentos Bomb

Instantly clears every ice cube on screen. Breathe easy — for a moment.

About SodaBro
